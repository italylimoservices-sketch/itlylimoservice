Choosing a private chauffeur for a Portofino trip isn't quite the same decision as choosing one for a big city. There's no metro system to compare against, no sprawling road network to worry about getting lost in. The decision is narrower and, in some ways, more specific: the right vehicle for your group, the right kind of booking for how you plan to move around, and a driver who understands that the village itself isn't somewhere you simply drive into and park.

Here's how to think through each of those pieces before booking.

## Start with Group Size and Luggage

Vehicle sizing is the most practical place to start, since it affects everything else about the booking. A couple or a solo traveler visiting Portofino for a day, or staying nearby for a few nights, is usually well served by an [executive sedan](/fleet/executive-sedan) or [luxury sedan](/fleet/luxury-sedan) — comfortable for up to three passengers and a reasonable amount of luggage, and easy to position near the village's limited drop-off points.

Once a group grows to four or five people, or the trip involves more luggage than a short visit would — say, Portofino is one stop on a longer Ligurian coast tour — a [luxury SUV](/fleet/luxury-suv) tends to be the better fit. Families or small groups of six or seven generally need an [executive van](/fleet/executive-van) or [luxury van](/fleet/luxury-van), both built to keep everyone together with room for luggage, which matters more on a multi-day trip than a single afternoon visit.

It's worth being honest about luggage when booking rather than rounding down. A vehicle that's technically tight but workable for passengers becomes a real problem once suitcases are added, particularly for a group arriving directly from an airport.

## Hourly Booking Versus Point-to-Point

This is one of the more consequential decisions for a Portofino trip, and it depends largely on what the day actually looks like.

A **point-to-point booking** — airport to hotel, hotel to the edge of Portofino and back — makes sense when the day has a clear structure and you know roughly how long you'll want to spend in the village. It's simpler to arrange and usually the more economical choice for a single transfer.

An **hourly or full-day booking** makes more sense when the plan is looser — when you want to linger in Portofino longer than expected, add a stop in Santa Margherita Ligure on the way, or decide on the day itself whether to continue further along the coast. Portofino has a way of absorbing more time than visitors expect, between the harbor views, the walk up to the castle area, and the narrow streets that reward slow wandering rather than a quick look. A fixed point-to-point transfer doesn't leave much room for that kind of day to breathe, while an hourly arrangement does.

If your visit is one stop within a longer day — say, a morning in Santa Margherita Ligure, an afternoon in Portofino, and a return to a hotel in the evening — hourly booking is generally the more sensible structure, since it keeps the same driver and vehicle available across the whole sequence rather than booking separate transfers for each leg.

## Local Road and Parking Knowledge Matters More Here

Portofino's access restrictions aren't a minor detail — they shape the entire visit. The village's streets near the harbor are narrow and largely pedestrian, and vehicles reach the edge of the village rather than its center. A chauffeur who already knows where vehicles can realistically stop, and how that changes during busier periods of the year, saves you from the awkward experience of arriving somewhere only to learn the car can't go any further and nobody planned for it.

This is a case where local familiarity genuinely outweighs general professionalism. A driver who is reliable and courteous but unfamiliar with Portofino specifically may still end up guessing at drop-off points or taking a less direct approach to the village's edge. When you're evaluating a chauffeur service for this particular destination, it's worth asking — even informally — whether the driver has handled Portofino drop-offs before, rather than assuming any competent driver in the region will know the village equally well.

## Think About What Happens After You're Dropped Off

Because the car can't follow you into the village itself, part of choosing a chauffeur service for Portofino is thinking through the handoff. Will the driver wait nearby, or do you need to arrange a specific pickup time and place for later? For a short visit, waiting nearby is usually straightforward. For a longer day, especially one that also includes a nearby town, it's worth discussing the pickup plan clearly rather than leaving it loose until you're actually back at the car.

This is also where hourly bookings tend to work better than a fixed point-to-point arrangement — the driver isn't tied to a single rigid schedule, and small adjustments to timing don't require renegotiating the whole booking.

## Matching the Booking to the Rest of Your Trip

Portofino rarely stands alone on an itinerary. Most visitors combine it with Genoa, a broader Ligurian coast tour, or a stop on the way to or from Cinque Terre or Milan. When choosing a chauffeur, it's worth thinking about the whole day or trip rather than just the Portofino leg in isolation — a driver booked for a full day covering multiple stops is a different arrangement than one booked purely for a Portofino round trip. ## Business Trips Need a Different Emphasis

If the visit is business-related — a client meeting that happens to be scheduled around Portofino, or an executive retreat using the village as a backdrop — the priority shifts. Reliability and punctuality matter more than flexibility, and a [corporate chauffeur](/corporate-chauffeur) arrangement, with a fixed schedule and a professional, quiet vehicle, tends to be the better structure than an open-ended hourly booking.

## Seasonal Timing Affects the Choice Too

Portofino draws noticeably more visitors in the warmer months, and that seasonal swing affects more than just how crowded the harbor feels. Drop-off points near the village edge can get busier, and a driver's familiarity with alternative approaches or quieter timing windows becomes more valuable during peak months than it would on a quiet weekday in the off-season. If your trip falls in the busier part of the year, it's worth asking your chauffeur service how they typically handle Portofino during that period, rather than assuming the same approach works year-round.

This also affects how loosely you should plan the day. In quieter months, a point-to-point booking with a fairly fixed schedule is easier to stick to. In peak season, building in some flexibility — even just an extra half hour on either end — tends to make the day less stressful, which is another point in favor of hourly booking when your visit falls in the busier stretch of the year.

## Don't Overlook Communication Before the Trip

A chauffeur service that asks good questions before the trip — about where you're staying, what else is on the day's itinerary, whether anyone in the group has mobility needs — is generally a better sign than one that simply confirms a pickup time and leaves the rest until the day itself. Portofino's layout means a few details genuinely do need sorting in advance: where exactly the car will wait, how you'll reconnect after walking into the village, and what the backup plan is if timing shifts. A service that raises these questions upfront, rather than leaving you to ask, is usually the more dependable choice.

## A Simple Way to Decide

If you're still unsure which arrangement fits, a few quick questions usually settle it:

- **How many people and how much luggage?** This settles the vehicle class.
- **Is the day tightly scheduled, or open to running long?** This settles point-to-point versus hourly.
- **Is Portofino the only stop, or one of several?** This settles whether you need a single transfer or a longer booking covering the full day.
- **Is the trip personal or business?** This settles how much weight to put on flexibility versus strict scheduling.

Once those four answers are clear, choosing a private chauffeur for Portofino becomes a much more straightforward decision.

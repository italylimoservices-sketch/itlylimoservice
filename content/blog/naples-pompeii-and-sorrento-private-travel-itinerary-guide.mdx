Landing at Naples Airport with Sorrento as your final destination opens up a natural opportunity that a lot of travelers overlook: stopping at Pompeii on the way rather than treating it as a separate day trip later in the visit. Because Pompeii sits roughly between Naples and Sorrento rather than off to one side, folding it into the arrival transfer makes more geographic sense here than it does for most other combinations — you're not adding a detour so much as pausing partway along a route you're already driving.

This is a different itinerary from combining Pompeii with the Amalfi Coast in a single day, which is a longer and more ambitious undertaking covered in our [Naples to Pompeii and Amalfi Coast day trip guide](/blog/naples-to-pompeii-and-amalfi-coast-day-trip-guide). Here, Sorrento isn't a stop along the way to somewhere else — it's the endpoint, the place you're actually staying, which changes the pacing considerably.

## Why Sorrento as the Endpoint Changes the Math

The Naples-Pompeii-Amalfi Coast day trip is demanding because it asks you to see two ambitious destinations and then return to a Naples base by evening, all within one day. A Naples-Pompeii-Sorrento itinerary is structurally easier, because Sorrento is where you're staying rather than where you're driving back from. Once you arrive in Sorrento in the afternoon or evening, the day is essentially over — there's no return drive to Naples to factor into the timing, and no pressure to leave Pompeii early to make sure you're back before dark. That single difference — an endpoint instead of a round trip — is what makes this combination noticeably more relaxed than its Amalfi Coast counterpart, even though Pompeii itself takes just as long to see properly either way.

## Starting Point: Naples Airport

Naples Airport is the nearest major airport to Sorrento, and the transfer between the two runs roughly 60 to 75 minutes under normal conditions when driven directly. Building a Pompeii stop into that transfer extends the day, of course, but it doesn't change the fundamental route — Pompeii sits close enough to the road between Naples and Sorrento that visiting it doesn't mean backtracking or adding a substantial detour. ## The Pompeii Stop

Pompeii is a large, well-known UNESCO-recognized archaeological site, and it rewards a genuine few hours of walking rather than a brief pass-through on the way to somewhere else. Because this itinerary treats Pompeii as a stop on the way to Sorrento rather than the main event of the day, it's worth being honest about the trade-off: you'll likely spend less time at the site than a dedicated Pompeii day trip would allow, but you'll also avoid the fatigue of trying to see it in full and still make a long return drive afterward. ## A Realistic Shape for the Day

Rather than a fixed hour-by-hour schedule, it helps to think of this itinerary as three loosely defined stretches. The first is the drive from Naples Airport toward Pompeii, which is short enough that it barely counts as a distinct leg. The second is the Pompeii visit itself, best treated as the anchor of the day and given real time rather than rushed. The third is the continuation from Pompeii to Sorrento, which — unlike the Amalfi Coast road beyond it — is a more direct stretch of driving rather than a long, winding coastal route.

Exact timing for all three stretches depends on traffic, the season, and how much of Pompeii you want to see, so it's worth treating any total figure as a rough shape rather than a number to hold your driver to. What matters more than a precise schedule is the order — airport, then Pompeii, then Sorrento — and giving the middle stretch the bulk of the day's time.

## Deciding If This Itinerary Suits Your Trip

This combination works best for travelers whose main base for the trip is Sorrento rather than Naples, and who would rather see Pompeii on arrival day than carve out a separate day for it once they've settled in. It's a noticeably less demanding version of pairing Pompeii with a major sight than the Naples-Pompeii-Amalfi Coast day trip, precisely because it ends with checking into a hotel rather than a long drive back. If your priority is spending as much unhurried time at Pompeii as possible, a dedicated day trip once you're already based in Sorrento may still suit you better — but for travelers looking to make efficient use of arrival day, folding Pompeii into the transfer from Naples Airport to Sorrento is a genuinely practical way to start a trip rather than losing a day to travel alone.

## What This Itinerary Doesn't Try to Be

It's worth being clear about what this route leaves out, since that's part of what keeps it manageable. This isn't an attempt to see Pompeii thoroughly, tour the Amalfi Coast, and reach Sorrento all in the same day — that's a considerably more ambitious undertaking, and one better suited to a dedicated day trip once you're already settled into a Sorrento base, using the [Amalfi Coast destination guide](/destinations/amalfi-coast) to help plan which towns to prioritize. The itinerary here is deliberately narrower in scope: one significant stop, folded into a transfer you're already making, ending the day in the place you're actually staying rather than driving back out again.

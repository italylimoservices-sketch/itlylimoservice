Leaving Sorrento for a flight out of Naples Airport is a straightforward drive on paper — a verified 60 to 75 minutes under normal conditions. In practice, the departure end of this trip has a few details worth planning for, particularly around timing and luggage, that are easy to overlook when you're focused on making the most of your last days on the coast.

## Distance and Timing

The drive from Sorrento to Naples Airport covers the same route as the inbound journey, just in reverse: along the base of the Sorrentine Peninsula, through smaller coastal towns, before joining the road toward the airport. The 60 to 75 minute figure is a reliable planning baseline, though traffic, time of day, and the season can push it toward the longer end of that range, particularly during peak summer travel periods.

## Building a Buffer Before Your Flight

The drive time alone shouldn't be the only number you're planning around. Once you arrive at Naples Airport, you'll still need time to check in (if you haven't already), pass through security, and get to your gate. For an international flight, airlines commonly recommend arriving around two to three hours before departure; domestic and short-haul European flights within the Schengen area often allow a bit less, but it's still worth building in a genuine margin rather than cutting it close.

Putting that together: for a flight departing at midday, a reasonable target might be leaving your Sorrento hotel around three to three and a half hours beforehand once you account for the drive, a margin for traffic, and standard airport arrival recommendations. Building in more time than feels strictly necessary is generally the safer approach, since the alternative — arriving at the airport later than planned — has far worse consequences than an extra 20 minutes spent at a gate.

## Departing From Your Hotel

One practical advantage of a private transfer for this leg is that it starts exactly where you are, rather than requiring you to first get yourself and your luggage to a train station or bus stop. Many hotels in Sorrento sit on narrow streets or along the cliffside with limited direct vehicle access, so it's worth confirming your exact hotel and any access notes when booking, so your driver knows the best pickup point in advance rather than working it out on the morning of departure.

## Luggage for the Trip Home

It's common to leave Sorrento with more luggage than you arrived with, between souvenirs, shopping, and the general accumulation that happens over a stay on the coast. A private transfer handles this without much friction — your driver loads bags directly at your hotel and unloads them again at the terminal, with no need to manage trolleys through a station or squeeze extra items into limited train storage.

If your luggage has grown noticeably since you arrived, it's worth letting your booking reflect that so the right vehicle is arranged. A car that comfortably fit your group on arrival might be tighter on the way out if you're bringing home considerably more than you brought.

## Choosing the Right Vehicle

As with the arrival leg, vehicle sizing matters for comfort and practicality. An [executive sedan](/fleet/executive-sedan) or [luxury sedan](/fleet/luxury-sedan) comfortably fits 3 passengers and 2 suitcases, suiting couples or solo travelers with standard luggage. Families or those with extra bags picked up during the trip often do better with a [luxury SUV](/fleet/luxury-suv), seating up to 5 passengers with 4 suitcases. Larger groups can use an [executive van](/fleet/executive-van) or [luxury van](/fleet/luxury-van), each fitting up to 7 passengers with 6 suitcases — a much easier way to keep a group and its accumulated luggage together than trying to coordinate multiple taxis or a crowded shared shuttle.

## The Drive Itself

Leaving Sorrento, the road initially winds through the town and nearby coastal villages before straightening out toward the airport. This final stretch along the peninsula tends to be the slower part of the journey, simply because of the terrain, while the later portion toward Naples moves more steadily. A driver who knows this specific route well tends to have a good sense of how traffic typically builds at different times of day, which is useful when you're working against a flight departure rather than an open-ended schedule.

## Why a Direct Transfer Suits This Leg Particularly Well

The alternative to a private transfer — a taxi from Sorrento, a shared shuttle bus, or a combination of the Circumvesuviana train and a connection into the airport — introduces more variables at exactly the point in your trip where you can least afford them. A missed shuttle departure, a crowded regional train, or simply not knowing exactly how long each leg of a multi-step journey will take is a bigger risk when there's a flight on the other end than it is on a more open-ended arrival day.

A private transfer removes that uncertainty. There's one pickup, one vehicle, and one destination, timed around your actual flight rather than a shared schedule. If your flight time shifts in the days before departure, adjusting a private pickup is generally far simpler than reworking train or shuttle bookings.

## Squeezing In a Last Stop on the Way

Some travelers ask whether it's realistic to fit in one more quick sight on the way from Sorrento to the airport — a coffee stop, a last view of the coast, or a brief detour. Whether this makes sense depends entirely on your buffer. If you've built in a generous margin before your flight, a short, pre-arranged stop can work. If your timeline is already tight against a flight departure, it's generally better to skip any add-on and go straight to the airport, since the consequences of missing a flight far outweigh the value of an extra twenty minutes somewhere along the road. If a stop interests you, it's worth discussing it honestly against your flight time when booking, rather than deciding spontaneously on the day.

## Early Morning Departures

A meaningful number of flights out of Naples Airport depart in the early morning, which means a correspondingly early pickup from Sorrento. This isn't a problem for a private transfer — drivers work around flight schedules rather than fixed operating hours — but it's worth confirming your pickup time clearly at booking and double-checking it the evening before, particularly for very early departures where there's less room to accommodate a mix-up. An early start is also one more reason to prefer a private pickup over a shared shuttle or public transport for this leg, since shuttle and train schedules are less likely to align conveniently with a pre-dawn airport arrival.

## Booking Ahead

As with any transfer tied to a flight, this leg works best when arranged in advance. Booking ahead means your flight number and departure time are on file, the right vehicle for your group and luggage is confirmed, and your driver can plan the pickup with an appropriate buffer built in — rather than you having to work out the timing yourself on your last morning in Sorrento.

## Final Thoughts

The Sorrento to Naples Airport leg is short by Italian travel standards, but it's also the leg with the least room for error if it ends with a flight to catch. Planning around a realistic total timeline — not just the 60 to 75 minute drive — and accounting for any extra luggage picked up along the way tends to make for a relaxed final morning rather than a rushed one.

Portofino's own sights can be covered in a few hours, which makes it a natural base for day trips rather than a place to spend several days on its own. The towns and cities within reach along this stretch of the Italian Riviera vary quite a bit in character and distance, and a private chauffeur changes which of them make sense as a comfortable day out versus a longer, more ambitious excursion.

## Santa Margherita Ligure: The Easiest Add-On

Santa Margherita Ligure sits just along the coast from Portofino and is the simplest day-trip pairing available. It's a larger, more relaxed town with its own seafront promenade, and it offers a different pace from Portofino's concentrated harbor scene. Because it's so close, Santa Margherita works well either as a lunch stop on a day centered on Portofino or as a destination in its own right for an afternoon of slower walking. There's no dedicated destination page for Santa Margherita on this site, but it fits easily into a Portofino-based day without adding much complexity.

## Rapallo: A Quieter, More Residential Stop

A little further along the coast from Santa Margherita, Rapallo offers yet another variation on the same stretch of Riviera — a town with its own harbor and seafront, generally quieter and less tourist-focused than Portofino itself. Rapallo tends to suit travelers who want to see how this part of Liguria lives day to day, away from the village that draws the heaviest crowds. Like Santa Margherita, Rapallo has no dedicated page here, but it pairs naturally with a longer Portofino-area day, particularly one that also includes Santa Margherita along the way.

## Genoa: A Bigger City, A Bigger Day

Genoa is a genuinely different kind of destination from the smaller coastal towns near Portofino — a major historic port city with a large old center, a working harbor, and a scale of sightseeing that a single village visit doesn't really prepare you for. A day trip to Genoa from a Portofino base is realistic, but it's worth treating as its own dedicated outing rather than something squeezed in alongside Santa Margherita and Rapallo on the same day. There's no verified, universal travel time between Portofino and Genoa that applies to every trip — road conditions and the time of day both affect it — so it's sensible to build in a generous window rather than assuming a quick back-and-forth. Our [destination page for Genoa](/destinations/genoa) covers what the city itself has to offer in more depth.

## Cinque Terre: Possible, But Worth an Honest Look First

Cinque Terre, the string of pedestrian villages further down the Ligurian coast, is sometimes mentioned in the same breath as Portofino because both are coastal highlights of this region. In practice, they sit a meaningful distance apart, and there's no verified door-to-door driving time that holds true for every visitor, since conditions along this coastline vary with season and traffic. A Cinque Terre day trip from Portofino is possible, but it deserves to be planned as a long day rather than a casual add-on, and it's worth going in with realistic expectations about how much time the drive itself will take out of the day. Once you arrive, Cinque Terre's villages are largely pedestrian and connected by local trains rather than roads, which changes the nature of sightseeing considerably compared to a chauffeured approach to Portofino itself. Our dedicated guide to [Portofino and Cinque Terre together](/blog/portofino-and-cinque-terre-private-chauffeur-travel-guide) covers this combination in much more detail, including how to sequence the two destinations sensibly.

## How a Chauffeur Supports a Multi-Stop Day

The real advantage of a private chauffeur on a day-trip itinerary isn't speed — it's continuity. Rather than treating each stop as a separate outing requiring its own parking search and logistics, a chauffeur carries the group between towns, handles the restricted access around Portofino's harbor on the way out, and keeps the day moving without the usual friction of relocating a self-driven car at every new stop. This matters more the more stops a day includes:

- A single add-on, like Santa Margherita, barely needs a chauffeur's help beyond the initial Portofino access
- A two-stop day including Rapallo benefits from not having to re-park at each town
- A full day trip to Genoa benefits from a driver who can manage the longer drive and the different parking realities of a larger city
- A Cinque Terre excursion benefits most of all, since the long drive portion is exactly the kind of stretch a chauffeur is best suited to absorb

## Structuring a Realistic Day

Not every day trip needs to be the most ambitious one available. A practical way to think about it:

- **Shorter days**: Portofino plus Santa Margherita, or Portofino plus Rapallo, both comfortable half-day or full-day combinations
- **Fuller days**: Portofino plus both Santa Margherita and Rapallo, using the chauffeur to move efficiently between all three
- **Dedicated excursions**: Genoa or Cinque Terre, each treated as its own full day given the distances involved, rather than combined with the smaller coastal towns on the same outing

Trying to force a Genoa or Cinque Terre excursion into the same day as Portofino's immediate neighbors tends to leave too little time for any of them. It's generally more satisfying to pick one ambition level for the day and stick with it.

## What to Prioritize if Time Is Limited

Not every visitor has the time for every option on this list, and it's worth thinking about priorities before committing to a plan. Travelers with only a single extra day beyond Portofino itself are usually better served by staying local — Santa Margherita and Rapallo together make for a full, satisfying day without the time pressure of a longer excursion. Travelers with a couple of extra days can afford to dedicate one of them to Genoa or Cinque Terre, treating the local towns as a separate, shorter outing on another day. Trying to see everything in one trip tends to work against the relaxed pace that makes this part of the coast appealing in the first place.
